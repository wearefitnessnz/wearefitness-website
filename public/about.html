My Story

The journey behind We Are Fitness.

My coaching style has been shaped by sport, challenge, change, and the belief that support can help people become more than they thought possible.

Where it all began

I’m Caro and sport has always been a huge part of my life. Not just something I do, but something I truly love.

My journey started young, inspired by my mum. She believed deeply in what sport can do, physically and mentally.

Sport became part of who I am

I started with synchronized swimming and represented Chile for almost 12 years.

Then I moved into water polo and represented Chile again — this time in Brazil.

Sport wasn’t just something I did… it became who I was.

Starting again in New Zealand

That journey brought me to New Zealand. And honestly… it was hard.

I arrived with zero English. I couldn’t find a job, hold conversations, or express myself.

I ended up cleaning toilets — because that was the only job I could get.

I stayed

I thought about giving up many times. But I didn’t.

I learned English. I built a life. I made a promise — if I do something, I’ll be the best at it.

Finding my path

I joined a gym… and everything changed.

I took my first Les Mills class and knew instantly — this is what I want to do.

It took almost 6 years to teach my first class. But I kept going.

Why this matters

Today, I’m still doing what I love.

It hasn’t been easy. But I can say this:

Working in what you love is possible. And it’s worth it.

That journey is the reason We Are Fitness exists.

Endurance Athlete

Leading by example.

Coaching is not just something I teach — it is something I live every day.

Sport has shaped who I am, from representing Chile in synchronized swimming and water polo, to building a life around endurance training and performance.

Over the years, I have challenged myself across running, triathlon, and long-course endurance events, including qualifying for the Ironman World Championship in Kona.

I understand the process — the discipline, the setbacks, the patience, and the consistency — because I live it too.

That experience helps me coach with empathy, structure, and a deep understanding of what it really takes to balance training with real life.
