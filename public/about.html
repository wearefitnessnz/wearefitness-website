My Story

More than fitness. This is my journey.

I’ve been meaning to properly introduce myself for a while now.

I’m Caro — and if you know me, you know that sport has always been a huge part of my life. Not just something I do, but something I truly love.

My journey started when I was very young, inspired by my mum. She believed deeply in what sport can do for someone’s life — not just physically, but mentally too. At home, there was one rule: learn how to swim properly, all four styles, and then you can choose your sport. And whatever you choose, you go all in.

So I did.

I started with synchronized swimming, and over time I became part of the Chilean national team, representing my country for almost 12 years. That chapter ended when I started university, but my love for sport did not. I moved into water polo, joined an incredible team, and again had the opportunity to represent Chile — this time in Brazil.

Sport was always there. But I still felt like something was missing.

After graduating, I made a decision that would change everything. I created a five-year plan to find my real passion. I always knew I wanted to work in something I truly loved — something that did not feel like work. A life where you do not need holidays from your job, because it is exactly where you want to be.

That journey brought me to New Zealand.

And honestly… it was hard.

I arrived with zero English. I could not find a job for months. I could not hold conversations, make friends, or even express myself. It was one of the most frustrating and lonely times of my life.

Despite having a degree and a strong background, I started cleaning toilets — because that was the only job I could get.

I thought about giving up many times. Going back home. Starting again somewhere easier. But I didn’t.

Because that dream — of finding my passion — was bigger.

So I stayed.

I learned English, which took years. I built a life. And I made a promise to myself: if I am going to do something, I will be the best at it.

That mindset opened doors — from cleaning roles into management, sponsorships, and slowly building a future here.

Then everything changed again.

I joined a gym.

Training had always been my safe place, but this time something clicked. I took my first Les Mills class and I knew instantly: this is it. This is what I want to do.

From that moment, I committed fully.

I went back to study. I completed my fitness diploma. I worked on my English. I fought for the visa that would allow me to work as a coach.

It took almost six years to teach my first class.

Six years of setbacks, leaving and coming back, doubting myself, being bullied, going through therapy, struggling mentally, losing my residency application during COVID, and losing more than $12,000 in the process.

I gave up more than once.

But one thing never changed: I never stopped training.

Training was always there — my grounding, my mental health, my identity. And when I could not believe in myself, someone else did.

My partner stood by me through everything and reminded me of what I was capable of, even when I could not see it.

And then things started to fall into place.

I finally got my opportunity. I taught my first class. I saw my photo go up on the instructor wall. I got my first job in the gym.

And from there, everything grew.

I was teaching more than 25 classes per week, working across multiple gyms, and finally living the life I had dreamed about for years.

Today, I’m still doing what I love.

It has not been easy. It has required sacrifice, resilience, and stepping far outside my comfort zone — leaving my country, my family, and everything familiar behind.

But I can honestly say this: working in what you love is possible. And it is worth it.

That journey is the reason We Are Fitness exists.

Because I know how hard it can feel to start, to believe in yourself, or to keep going when things are not working. And I also know what happens when you do not give up.

That is what I want to help people experience.

What drives it

Warm coaching. Honest support. Real-life progress.

We Are Fitness was built from lived experience — not just study, not just theory, but years of learning what it means to keep going, adapt, and grow.

I want people to feel supported from the beginning, confident in the process, and proud of what they are capable of building over time.

Endurance Athlete

Leading by example.

Coaching is not just something I teach — it is something I live every day.

Sport has shaped who I am, from representing Chile in synchronized swimming and water polo, to building a life around endurance training and performance.

Over the years, I have challenged myself across running, triathlon, and long-course endurance events, including qualifying for the Ironman World Championship in Kona.

I understand the process — the discipline, the setbacks, the patience, and the consistency — because I live it too.

That experience helps me coach with empathy, structure, and a deep understanding of what it really takes to balance training with real life.
